Frequently asked

How can I edit text in a PDF?Click any line of existing text to edit it in place, or use Add Text to place new text anywhere on the page.
Can I edit a PDF without converting it to Word?Yes — this opens and edits the original PDF directly, then exports a real PDF, no round-trip through Word.
Can I edit a scanned PDF?Scanned pages can be made editable with built-in OCR text detection, flagged automatically when a page has no selectable text.
Is my uploaded PDF stored anywhere?No. Everything happens locally in your browser tab; nothing is uploaded or saved on a server.
